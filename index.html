Floating Panda Dream

A small Panda3D arcade MVP where you pilot a floating panda through a dreamy sky lane, collect moon candy, chain lantern rings, and dodge ghost clouds before the timer runs out.

Open sourceView game code
P

Collect

Moon candy gives points and lantern rings boost your streak for bigger scores.

Dodge

Ghost clouds drain energy and break streaks, so clean movement matters.

Controls

Use WASD or arrows to float, Space to dash, R to restart, and Esc to quit.

Run Locally

pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/main.py
